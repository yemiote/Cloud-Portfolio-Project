My goal was to learn how to host a static website on AWS S3. I started by creating a bucket, uploading files, and enabling static hosting. Initially, I faced access denial and permission issues, which I solved by adjusting the bucket policies.

This experience helped me understand how static hosting on S3 works and deepened my knowledge of bucket policy configurations. It boosted my confidence and gave me a skill I can use to help businesses establish their online presence with ease.
